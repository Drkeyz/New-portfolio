<Section n="02" title="First impression" headline="Feel the problem *first*, then find a way to help.">

The home page had one job: make a visitor who knows nothing about the foundation feel the weight of the problem, then make it easy to do something about it. Real faces, clear numbers and a single primary action carry that, in that order.

<Section n="03" title="The site" headline="Five pages, *one* coherent story.">

Every page opens with the same lavender hero and a single clear heading, then gets out of the way of the photographs. Visitors always know where they are, and every page ends with a way to help.
