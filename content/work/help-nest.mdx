<Section n="01" title="Context" headline="Every escalation *started from zero.*">

<Lead>B2B companies running several products face a compound support problem.</Lead>

Bots resolve the common questions. But the moment a conversation needs nuance, the handoff to a human erases everything before it. Agents walk into escalations blind, managers can't see which products cause the most friction, and customers feel the gap.

Helpnest was designed from the ground up to close that gap, by treating the **bot-to-human transition** as the core design problem.

<Statement text="The best escalation is the one the customer *never notices* has happened." cite="Design principle · Helpnest" />

</Section>

<Findings

<Lead>Each failure is a transition: bot to human, knowledge to chat, individual to team, reactive to proactive. Helpnest is designed around those transitions.</Lead>
