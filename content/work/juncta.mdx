<Section n="01" title="Context" headline="A protocol where *capital never sits* idle.">

<Lead>Every Move-based DEX launching was a concentrated liquidity market maker: a port of the Uniswap V3 playbook.</Lead>

LPs set static price ranges, wait, and watch their capital sit idle the moment price drifts outside them. The architecture has no idea which bins are working and which aren't.

Juncta went **DLMM** instead, for architectural reasons rather than cosmetic ones. Liquidity lives in discrete price bins, and the protocol knows which bin is active at every moment. That awareness is what makes the core mechanic clean: liquidity in inactive bins is routed into the lending market automatically.
